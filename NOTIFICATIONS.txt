SRI NEWS notification setup

- Firebase Cloud Messaging topic: all_news
- Notification permission is requested on startup.
- Foreground FCM messages are displayed as Android notifications.
- Background notification messages are handled by FCM.
- The app subscribes every installation to all_news.
- To send a notification immediately, Firebase Console can send a
  notification to topic all_news.
- Automatic notification on every Firestore news publish requires a
  trusted server/Cloud Function/other backend to send FCM; do not put
  Firebase server credentials inside the mobile app.
